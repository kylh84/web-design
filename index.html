Nature Moments Gallery

This gallery showcases beautiful outdoor scenes from around the world, including mountains, lakes, forests, beaches, waterfalls, and desert landscapes. The goal of this site is to present a clean, responsive, and accessible photo collection that works well across mobile, tablet, and large desktop screens.

The layout changes based on screen size and also responds to user preferences such as reduced motion and dark color scheme settings. Each image includes alternative text to support accessibility and help all visitors better understand the content of the gallery.
